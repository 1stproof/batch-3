This problem turns out to admit a solution using existing work in the literature. Essentially, a solution may be found by combining a paper of Connes \& Consani and a paper of Hall; the former of which is directly about hyperfields. Once these two papers have been found, no great effort is required to solve the problem, more on the level of a challenging homework problem (perhaps 2 pages once definitions have been given) than a research question. The review should be read in that light.
