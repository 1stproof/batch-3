\begin{question}
Let $(M,\omega)$ be a closed symplectic manifold and $D\subset M$ a symplectic
simple normal crossings divisor. Does there exist an $\omega$-tame almost
complex structure $J$ such that locally on $M$ there are smooth complex
coordinates $(z_1,\ldots,z_n)$ in which, for some $0\le r\le n$, the functions
$z_1,\ldots,z_r$ are $J$-holomorphic and $D$ is given by
\[
z_1\cdots z_r=0?
\]
Here one may first replace $\omega$ by a symplectic form connected to it
through a path of symplectic forms, all cohomologous to $\omega$, along
which $D$ remains a symplectic simple normal crossings divisor.
\end{question}
