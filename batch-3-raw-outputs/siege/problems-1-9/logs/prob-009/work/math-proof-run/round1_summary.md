## Goal
As posed: for the holomorphic, smoothly trivial family V_t (t in the disc) deforming a smooth 2-dimensional totally geodesic V_0 in M_g, with hypotheses (i) (boundary pieces in each stratum form smoothly trivial families) and (ii) (compatibility of "arises-from-closure" relations between strata), if ∂V_t = closure(V_t) ∩ Δ_Γ is 1-dimensional and π_i(∂V_t) = {Y_t} for all t, then Y_t = Y_0 for all t. Ledger [GOAL] line is OPEN.

## Planner's initial impressions (unverified)
- t -> Y_t is a holomorphic map from the disc to M_{g_i,n_i}; the task is to show it is constant. Nothing soft (compactness, holomorphy) obviously forces this, so the rigidity must come from the totally geodesic structure of V_0 combined with (i),(ii).
- ∂V_0 cannot arise as the closure of a 1-dim boundary piece in another stratum (dimension would drop), so ∂V_0 is a component of the boundary divisor of the closure of V_0; hypothesis (ii) presumably acts on the 0-dimensional boundary points of the curve ∂V_t (points of closure(∂V_t) in deeper strata) and their relation to other boundary curves.
- Heuristic from flat geometry: V_0 totally geodesic of dim 2 corresponds (Wright) to a GL(2,R)-orbit closure M of complex dim 4, rank 2. A factor of a boundary point on which the limiting (WYSIWYG, Mirzakhani–Wright / Chen–Wright) differential is nonzero cannot have constant projection along a boundary component (Teichmüller flow moves it), so the constant factor Y_0 is plausibly a component where the limit differential vanishes (lower level in the multi-scale compactification). How this pins Y_0 is unclear.
- Possible issue: statement may hide an assumption; whether the claim can fail must be examined.

## Open gaps
1. Any mechanism forcing t -> Y_t constant.
2. Structure of the boundary of a 2-dim totally geodesic V_0 along a component constant in one factor.
