Context from earlier work on this problem (established by previous attempts; check anything you rely on):
1. t -> Y_t is a holomorphic map from the disc into M_{g_i,n_i}. (Reading: closure(𝒱) in M̄_g × Δ is analytic and its fibre over t is closure(V_t); then 𝒲 = closure(𝒱) ∩ (Δ_Γ × Δ) is analytic, its image under π_i × id is the graph {(Y_t,t)}, analytic by Remmert's proper mapping theorem on compact closures, and an analytic graph over a disc is the graph of a holomorphic map.) So the claim is equivalent to dY_t/dt ≡ 0, or to Y_t ranging over a countable set.
2. Writing ∂V_t = {Y_t} × C_t with C_t a curve in the other factors, the closure of ∂V_t in deeper strata is {Y_t} × closure(C_t): factor i never degenerates along it, and hypothesis (ii) applied to Δ_Γ and strata in its closure holds tautologically whatever Y_t is — it gives no constraint.
3. ∂V_t is a boundary curve of the compact surface closure(V_t), not the closure of a boundary piece in another stratum; so (ii) only constrains how the finitely many deeper points {Y_t} × (closure(C_t) \ C_t) lie on the closures of OTHER boundary curves of V_t.
4. Purely local arguments near ∂V_t cannot work: in coordinates (y on M_{g_i,n_i}, z on other factors, plumbing parameters s), a germ y = Y_t + O(σ) with Y_t an arbitrary holomorphic arc satisfies every local constraint that (i),(ii) impose near ∂V_t.
5. If every V_t were totally geodesic, the claim would follow: totally geodesic subvarieties are countable (Wright's correspondence with GL(2,R)-orbit closures + Eskin–Mirzakhani–Mohammadi countability), so a continuous family of them is constant.
6. Heuristics at t = 0 (from memory, unverified): by Wright, V_0 is the image of an orbit closure M of quadratic differentials with dim_C M = 4, rank 2; the boundary curve ∂V_0 corresponds (Chen–Wright WYSIWYG / multi-scale differentials of Bainbridge–Chen–Gendron–Grushevsky–Möller) to a rank-1 boundary orbit closure on the top-level components; since Y_0 does not move along ∂V_0, its limit differential is zero (Y_0 lies at a lower level), and plausibly (Y_0, lower-level differential) is rigid. The difficulty: for t ≠ 0, V_t carries no flat structure, so rigidity at t = 0 must be transported to V_t by something that hypotheses (i),(ii) and the smooth triviality preserve.
