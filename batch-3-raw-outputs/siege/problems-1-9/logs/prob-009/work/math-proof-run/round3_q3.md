Open question attempted: ledger's open question "does a 2-dimensional totally geodesic V_0 admit any nontrivial holomorphic deformation V_t?", via deformation theory — prove infinitesimal rigidity of closure(V_0) as a stratified (log) subvariety of M̄_g, or at least that every deformation fixes the constant boundary factor Y_0 to all orders.

Context from earlier work on this problem (established or observed by previous attempts; re-check anything you rely on):
1. t -> Y_t is a holomorphic map from the disc into M_{g_i,n_i}. (Reading adopted: closure(𝒱) in M̄_g × Δ is analytic and its fibre over t is closure(V_t); then closure(𝒱) ∩ (Δ_Γ × Δ) is analytic, its image under π_i × id is the graph {(Y_t,t)}, analytic by Remmert's proper mapping theorem, and an analytic graph over a disc is the graph of a holomorphic map.) Hence the claim is equivalent to dY_t/dt ≡ 0, or to Y_t ranging over a countable set; and by the identity theorem it suffices to show Y_t is constant for t near 0.
2. Writing ∂V_t = {Y_t} × C_t with C_t a curve in the product of the other factors, the closure of ∂V_t in deeper strata is {Y_t} × closure(C_t): factor i never degenerates along it, and hypothesis (ii) applied to Δ_Γ and strata in its closure holds tautologically whatever Y_t is.
3. ∂V_t is a boundary curve (a component of the boundary divisor) of the compact surface closure(V_t), not the closure of a boundary piece lying in another stratum; so (ii) only has content at the finitely many deeper points {Y_t} × (closure(C_t) \ C_t), where it says that if at t = 0 such a point lies (locally) on the closure of ANOTHER boundary piece of V_0, the same incidence holds for all t.
4. Purely local arguments near ∂V_t cannot work: in coordinates (y on M_{g_i,n_i}, z on other factors, plumbing parameters s), a germ y = Y_t + O(|s|) with Y_t an arbitrary holomorphic arc satisfies every local constraint that (i),(ii) impose near ∂V_t. Likewise the monodromy around ∂V_t (products of Dehn twists about the curves pinched in Γ, which act trivially on the subsurface carrying Y_t) and self-intersection numbers of boundary curves on closure(V_t) are insensitive to moving Y_t. Any proof must use a GLOBAL property of closure(V_t).
5. Topological constancy: reading smooth triviality together with (i),(ii) as a stratified homeomorphism closure(V_0) × Δ ≅ closure(𝒱) over Δ, the monodromy representation ρ: π_1(V_t) → Mod(S_g) → Sp(2g,Z), all local monodromies around boundary curves, and the weight filtrations of limiting mixed Hodge structures are independent of t. Moreover any integer-valued characteristic number that varies continuously with t (Chern numbers of Deligne extensions of Hodge bundles of a sub-VHS that persists over all V_t; Chern numbers of log tangent bundles of (closure(V_t), boundary divisor) when the boundary is normal crossing and the pair is smoothly trivial; Euler/Toledo numbers of flat bundles) is independent of t.
6. If every V_t were totally geodesic, the claim would follow: totally geodesic subvarieties of M_g are countable (Wright: they are images of GL(2,R)-orbit closures; Eskin–Mirzakhani–Mohammadi: countably many orbit closures), so a continuous family of them is constant. Similarly if V_0 is rigid (admits no nontrivial deformation of the kind described), the claim holds trivially. The claim is therefore implied by either (a) "V_t is totally geodesic for all t" or (b) rigidity of V_0; neither is known.
7. Heuristics at t = 0 (from memory, unverified): by Wright, V_0 is the image of an orbit closure M of (quadratic or abelian) differentials, dim_C M = 4, rank 2, rel 0; at X ∈ V_0 the differentials ω with (X,ω) ∈ M span a 2-dim space E_X, and W = E ⊕ \bar E is plausibly a flat rank-4 sub-local system (sub-VHS of Hodge numbers (2,2), Filip). The boundary curve ∂V_0 corresponds (Chen–Wright WYSIWYG; multi-scale differentials of Bainbridge–Chen–Gendron–Grushevsky–Möller) to a rank-1 boundary orbit closure (a Teichmüller curve) on top-level components; Y_0 lies at a lower level (its top-level differential is zero), and plausibly (Y_0, lower-level differential) is rigid (in covering-construction examples Y_0 is a cover of a 3-pointed P^1, i.e. a Belyi curve with no moduli). Over V_t, t ≠ 0, there is no flat structure.
8. Hodge transport so far (unfinished): W persists topologically over every V_t; by Deligne semisimplicity it underlies a sub-VHS over V_t if it is an isotypic component of multiplicity one; but the limiting Hodge filtration along ∂V_t varies holomorphically with t, so a countability conclusion needs an algebraic or numerical condition that is preserved for all t.

Reduction to use (verify it): work on a finite cover of M̄_g by a fine moduli space (level structure ≥ 3) or on the stack. Let S = closure(V_0) with boundary divisor D. A family as in the problem (closures analytic, stratification smoothly trivial by (i),(ii)) is a holomorphic map from the disc to the Douady space (or Hilbert scheme) of compact subspaces of M̄_g, in fact to the sub-space of LOG/stratum-preserving deformations, whose tangent space at [S] is H^0(S, N^{log}), N^{log} = T_{M̄_g}(-log ∂M̄_g)|_S / T_S(-log D) (make this precise, including at points where S is not transverse to the strata). If H^0(S, N^{log}) = 0, the germ of that space at [S] is a (possibly non-reduced) point, so every family through [S] parametrised by a reduced disc is constant near t = 0, and then Y_t ≡ Y_0 (identity theorem). A weaker sufficient statement: the map from the germ of the deformation space to M_{g_i,n_i}, [S_t] ↦ Y_t, is constant — note that vanishing of its DIFFERENTIAL at [S] alone does not suffice (Y_t = Y_0 + t^2 v is possible), so one needs either H^0 = 0 or an all-orders argument.
