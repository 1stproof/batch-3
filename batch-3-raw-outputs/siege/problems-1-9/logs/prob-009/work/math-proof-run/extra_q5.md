# Task: continuity of the "subsurface restriction with boundary lengths set to 0" on augmented Teichmüller space

This is a self-contained question about augmented Teichmüller space. It is one cited input (labelled (Ψ)) of a long conditional argument about the problem stated after this text, but you do NOT need to solve that problem. Your only job is the statement below.

## Setting and notation

- S is a closed oriented surface of genus g ≥ 2; T_g its Teichmüller space; T̄_g the augmented Teichmüller space (Abikoff/Bers): points are marked stable (noded) hyperbolic surfaces, obtained by pinching a multicurve; the topology is the one in which X_k → z̃ iff the lengths of the node curves of z̃ tend to 0 and, after suitable conjugation, holonomies restricted to the complementary subsurfaces converge to type-preserving holonomies of the components of z̃ (equivalently: Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates for a pants decomposition containing the node curves converge, with lengths of node curves → 0 and their twist parameters unconstrained). Mod_g acts; T̄_g/Mod_g = M̄_g.
- Σ′ ⊂ S is an essential subsurface (compact, each boundary curve essential, possibly with several boundary components, not an annulus or pair of pants unless stated), with boundary curves ∂Σ′ = {γ_1,…,γ_r}. Σ′° is Σ′ with each boundary curve replaced by a puncture, labelled by that curve; T(Σ′°) its Teichmüller space; M̄(Σ′°) the Deligne–Mumford compactification of the moduli space of Σ′° with labelled punctures; ∂M̄(Σ′°) its boundary (noded curves).
- P′ is a pants decomposition of Σ′ (interior curves only). For X ∈ T_g, ψ_{Σ′,P′}(X) ∈ T(Σ′°) is the point whose Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates with respect to P′ (lengths and twists of the interior curves of P′, twists measured with respect to a fixed choice of seams in Σ′) are those of X restricted to Σ′, while the boundary lengths ℓ(γ_1),…,ℓ(γ_r) are set to 0 (cusps). [ψ] denotes the image in M̄(Σ′°).

## Statement to prove (Ψ)

(i) ψ_{Σ′,P′} : T_g → T(Σ′°) is continuous, and natural: ψ_{hΣ′,hP′}(hX) = h_*ψ_{Σ′,P′}(X) for h ∈ Mod_g.

(ii) Let X_k → z̃ in T̄_g, and suppose every curve of ∂Σ′ is a node curve of z̃. Then [ψ_{Σ′,P′}(X_k)] converges in M̄(Σ′°) to the class of the Σ′-side of z̃ (the noded surface formed by the components of z̃ lying in Σ′, with the nodes on ∂Σ′ turned into labelled punctures; this may itself be noded if z̃ has node curves inside Σ′), and the limit does not depend on P′. If moreover some essential non-peripheral curve of Σ′ has length → 0 along X_k, the limit lies in ∂M̄(Σ′°).

(iii) (chart form) Let J be a stable curve, U a precisely invariant neighbourhood of a lift J̃ ∈ T̄_g (stabiliser Stab(J̃), an extension of Aut(J) by the free abelian group Tw generated by Dehn twists about the node curves α_e of J̃), with U/Tw ≅ B ⊂ Def(J) (the Kuranishi/plumbing chart, coordinates (z, s_e)). For a vertex y of the dual graph of J let Σ_y be the corresponding component of S ∖ ∪α_e, and L_y := {s_e = 0 for all edges e at y} ⊂ B. Then [ψ_{Σ_y}] (for any pants decomposition of Σ_y) is Tw-invariant, so descends to a function on the image of U in B; it extends continuously to a neighbourhood of L_y in B (in the sense: along any sequence in U converging in T̄_g to a point over L_y, [ψ_{Σ_y}] converges), and equals [F_y] on L_y, where F_y(p) is the component labelled y of the fibre over p, with its special points.

## Known caveat to address

A delicate case is (ii) when some curves of P′ cross node curves of z̃ lying inside Σ′. In the application this case never arises in the limit (in the application, no node curve of z̃ lies inside Σ′ whenever the "independence of P′" is used), so you may prove (ii) under the extra hypothesis "no node curve of z̃ lies in the interior of Σ′" for the convergence/independence part, and prove the "limit lies in ∂M̄(Σ′°)" part separately (there, it suffices to show: if an interior curve's length → 0 then [ψ(X_k)] leaves every compact subset of M(Σ′°)). State precisely which version you prove.

## Hints

- The FN-to-hyperbolic-structure map extends real-analytically to boundary lengths ≥ 0 (lengths 0 = cusps); trace (Fricke) coordinates of the pants groups are analytic in cosh(ℓ/2), sinh(ℓ/2), and the twists. Changing P′ to P″ is a real-analytic change of coordinates depending continuously on the boundary lengths down to 0.
- For (ii): as ℓ(γ_j) → 0 the collar lemma separates Σ′ from the rest; compare ψ(X_k) with the actual hyperbolic surface X_k|_Σ′ (bordered, geodesic boundary of length ℓ_k → 0) — both have the same interior FN coordinates; show their "difference" tends to 0 in the sense needed (e.g. a (1+o(1))-bi-Lipschitz or quasiconformal comparison on thick parts), and use convergence of holonomies on π_1(Σ′) given by the definition of the T̄_g topology.
- Mumford compactness: a sequence in M(Σ′°) leaves compact sets iff its systole → 0; the systole of ψ(X_k) is comparable to the shortest interior curve of X_k|_Σ′ when the boundary is short (collar lemma).
- (iii) follows from (i), (ii) and the description of U/Tw (plumbing coordinates; twisting about α_e acts on T(Σ_y°) by a mapping class of Σ_y° — in fact trivially on the class in moduli space — since α_e is peripheral in Σ_y).

A complete proof of (Ψ) (or of the clearly stated weakened version), with each cited classical fact stated precisely (Abikoff's description of T̄_g, collar lemma, Mumford compactness, Wolpert's/Fricke's analyticity of FN coordinates). If some part cannot be proved, say exactly which, and whether it could be false. Do not overclaim.
