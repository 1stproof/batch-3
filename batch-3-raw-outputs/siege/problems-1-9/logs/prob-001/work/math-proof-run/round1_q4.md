Open question attempted: the combinatorial core of the headline claim for finitely generated P -- prove the finite-poset acyclicity statement, and the reduction of birhizomic cohomology at the terminal object P to it.

Part A (pure combinatorics, prove completely). Let sigma be a strictly convex rational polyhedral cone, Faces(sigma) its face poset, Sigma a rational fan subdivision of sigma (support |Sigma| = sigma), and r : Sigma -> Faces(sigma) the monotone map sending a cone c to the smallest face of sigma containing c. Let G : Faces(sigma)^op -> Ab be any functor (maps G(tau) -> G(tau') for tau' a face of tau). Prove that the derived limit R lim over Sigma^op of c |-> G(r(c)) is G(sigma) concentrated in degree 0 (equivalently, the sheaf cohomology on the finite Alexandrov space Sigma, whose open sets are subfans, of the pullback r^{-1}G equals G(sigma) in degree 0). Suggested tools: Quillen's Theorem A / homotopy cofinality for derived limits, with the needed contractibility of comma posets proved via convexity (open stars of relative interiors of faces); or an induction on dim sigma using the stratification by faces. Be careful with the direction of comma categories.
Part B (the reduction). Let P be a fine saturated sharp monoid, sigma = Hom(P, R_{>=0}). Show that the birhizomic site of P (the category A_P^op of homomorphisms P -> Q with the birhizomic topology described in the problem) has the property that H^i(P, pi^{-1}F) = colim over subdivisions Sigma of sigma of H^i(Sigma, r^{-1}F) -- or whatever correct comparison holds -- where a presheaf F on B_P^op is viewed as a functor on Faces(sigma) via P[-x]^sharp <-> the face x^perp of sigma. You will need: that subdivision charts form birhizomic covers; that every birhizomic cover of P is refined by one coming from a subdivision (together with localizations); a Cech-to-derived argument (e.g. the subdivision covers form a cofinal system and their Cech nerves are computed by the finite posets above); and that the sheafification in pi^{-1} does not change the values on chart objects in a way that breaks this. Make every step rigorous, and flag precisely any step you cannot complete.

Background heuristics from the director (UNVERIFIED -- check anything you use):
(a) A_P^op has a terminal object, id: P -> P (P = P[-0]^sharp lies in B_P). Likewise in B_P^op the object P is terminal, so for any presheaf F on B_P^op, global sections are F(P) and higher cohomology (trivial topology) vanishes. For the object P[-x]^sharp, the relevant comparison is with the same question for the monoid P[-x]^sharp (A_{P[-x]^sharp} is a slice of A_P). So the question amounts to: (i) the counit/unit identifies (pi^{-1}F)(P[-x]^sharp) with F(P[-x]^sharp) on sections, and (ii) H^i(A_P^op-site, pi^{-1}F) evaluated at the terminal object vanishes for i>0 and equals F(P) for i=0 -- for every P (then apply to the localizations).
(b) Saturated + sharp implies P^gp is torsion-free and the partial order on P^gp is isolated, so it extends to a total order; hence there is always a valuation v : P -> V with v^{-1}(0) = 0. So no family of maps P -> P[-x_i]^sharp with all x_i nonzero is a birhizomic cover; the Zariski-type covers inside B_P are trivial (each P[-x]^sharp is sharp, "local").
(c) Expected picture for P finitely generated (fine, saturated, sharp): let sigma = Hom(P, R_{>=0}) be the dual cone. Objects P[-x]^sharp of B_P correspond to faces x^perp of sigma; a presheaf F on B_P^op is a functor on faces with restriction maps from a face to its subfaces. Birhizomic covers should include the covers of P by the charts of a subdivision Sigma of sigma (log blow-ups / log modifications), and points of the topos should be valuations P -> V (possibly of higher rank) up to a suitable equivalence. For a subdivision Sigma with r : Sigma -> faces(sigma) sending a cone to the smallest face containing it, one expects R lim over Sigma^op of c |-> F(r(c)) to equal F(sigma) in degree 0 (a Quillen theorem A / convexity argument: the cones c with r(c) containing a fixed face tau form a poset whose realization is the open star of the relative interior of tau, which is contractible). If the birhizomic cohomology at P is the colimit over subdivisions of these finite-poset cohomologies, the answer would be YES for finitely generated P. Non-finitely-generated P (e.g. irrational cones, P = Q_{>=0}, rank-two monoids with non-principal faces, infinite-rank monoids) and the role of higher-rank valuations / the "infinitesimal" condition are where this picture is least certain.
