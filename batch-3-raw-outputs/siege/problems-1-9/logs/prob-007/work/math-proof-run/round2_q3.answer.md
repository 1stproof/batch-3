## 1. The forgetful map W → M_{0,4}(a_0,a_2,a_3,v) (PROVED by computation)
Send (0,1,t) to (0,1,∞) using m(z) = z(1−t)/(z−t), so m(0)=0, m(1)=1, m(t)=∞. With P=t²−3t+1, Q=t²+t−1, R=3t²−3t+1 and v=−P²/D, v−t=−R²/D (background item (2)):
  u(t) := m(v(t)) = (1−t)P²/R².
Check: (1−t)P² − R² = −t⁵−2t⁴+t³+2t²−t = −t(t²+t−1)² (expand P²=t⁴−6t³+11t²−6t+1, R²=9t⁴−18t³+15t²−6t+1). Hence
  u − 1 = −t Q²/R².
So u is a rational map of degree 5 with these fibres:
  u=0: t=1 (simple), roots of P (double);  u=1: t=0 (simple), roots of Q (double);  u=∞: t=∞ (simple, 5−4=1), roots of R (double).
Riemann–Hurwitz: 2·5−2 = 8, and these fibres account for 6, so there are 2 more simple critical points of u, outside these fibres. Also u(1/2) = (1/2)(1/16)/(1/16) = 1/2, which is not a puncture of the u-plane.

## 2. Consequence: the quotient F̄ gives no obstruction for the {v,a_j} curves (dead end)
The image of G_0 in PMod(S², {a_0,a_2,a_3,v}) ≅ π_1(C∖{0,1}) (≅ F̄, the point-push group of v) is u_*(π_1 W). The image of K is u_*(N). Here N is normally generated by the loops around t=1/2 and around the six roots. Its image is generated as follows:
- the loop around t=1/2 maps to a null-homotopic loop, since u(1/2)=1/2 is not a puncture;
- the loops around the six roots map to conjugates of the squares of the loops around 0, 1 and ∞, i.e. of ℓ_0², ℓ_2², ℓ_3².

Sketched lemma: u_*(π_1 W) = π_1(C∖{0,1}). Reason: the simple preimages t=1, 0, ∞ are punctures of W. Their small loops map to conjugates of single generator loops around 0, 1, ∞. Because u is a connected branched cover, these conjugates should generate the whole group. I did not check this fully.

If the lemma holds, the image of K in F̄ is the full normal closure of ℓ_0², ℓ_2², ℓ_3². The quotient F̄/<<ℓ_0², ℓ_2², ℓ_3²>> with ℓ_0ℓ_2ℓ_3 = 1 is the Klein four-group, which is finite. Every conjugate of ℓ_j² dies in it. So the image of T_γ² in F̄ lies in the image of K for every curve γ around {v,a_j}, and this quotient cannot detect an infinite orbit. Deciding the {v,a_j} family needs finer information about K inside F itself. That question is open.

## 3. Candidate answer (CONJECTURAL)
Take γ to be a curve around the two critical values {a_1, v} with the "3/0 split" of background item (6). Then T_γ lifts to a twist that is trivial relative to A, so T_γ ∈ G. Concrete choice: γ = T_β²(γ0), where
- γ0 is the cusp curve at t=1/2 (background item (4));
- β is any curve around {v, a_0}, so T_β² ∈ G by item (5).

Then T_γ = T_β² T_{γ0} T_β^{−2}. It preserves D_f and fixes the component T_β²·D_0.

Claimed but NOT proved: some component (equivalently, after conjugating, D_0) has an infinite ⟨T_γ⟩-orbit. By background item (7) this is the statement that no nonzero power of T_γ lies in K. In the quotient Q ≅ Z/2*Z/2*Z/2 of background item (8), this reduces to showing that a specific parabolic conjugate of ℓ̄_1 is not in the subgroup generated by the G_0-orbit of x̄_{1/2}. That needs the action of G_0 on Q, which I have not computed.
