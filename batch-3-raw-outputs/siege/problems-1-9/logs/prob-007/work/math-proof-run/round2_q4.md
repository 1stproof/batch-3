(1) Lifting framework (to be justified). If φ ∈ PMod(S^2,B) has a lift φ~ (f∘φ~ = φ∘f) fixing A pointwise, then f^*(φ·τ) = φ~·f^*(τ) and i_*(φ·τ) = i(φ)·i_*(τ). Let G = {φ : some such lift has φ~ = i(φ) in PMod(S^2,A)}; G preserves D_f. Conversely if τ and φ·τ both lie in D_f then φ ∈ G (both markings conjugate f to the same rational map). So with W := image of D_f in M_{0,5}, D_f/G ≅ W, G permutes the components transitively, and if D_0 is a component with stabilizer G_0 then π_0(D_f) ≅ G/G_0 and G_0 = image of the monodromy π_1(W) → π_1(M_{0,5}) = PMod(S^2,B) (D_0 → W is a covering since PMod(S^2,B) acts freely). The ⟨φ⟩-orbit of D_0 is infinite iff no nonzero power of φ lies in G_0. (Since degree 2 and the two critical values a_1, v are both marked, every φ ∈ PMod(S^2,B) lifts, uniquely up to the deck involution.)
