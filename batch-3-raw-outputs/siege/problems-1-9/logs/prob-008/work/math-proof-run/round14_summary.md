**Missing statement (SC) (summable su-chains returning to the weak-stable set).** Let M be a compact connected smooth manifold of dimension n >= 2 with a Riemannian distance d, X a smooth vector field on M with flow g_t (NO hypoellipticity assumed), nu a smooth positive density, d_X := div_nu X and J_t(x) := int_0^t d_X(g_s x) ds. Let mu be a Borel probability measure invariant and ergodic for g_1 with h_mu(g_1) > 0 and int J_1 dmu = 0. Then there exist constants C >= 1, theta > 0, eta in [0, theta), C' >= 0, a point x_0 in M and two sequences (a_i)_{i>=0}, (b_i)_{i>=0} of points of M with a_0 = b_0 = x_0 such that each sequence (x_i) in {(a_i), (b_i)} satisfies, for every i >= 0:
 (L1) d(g_t x_{2i}, g_t x_{2i+1}) <= C e^{-theta|t|} d(x_{2i}, x_{2i+1}) for all t <= 0;

**Why it should hold (planner sketch, round 14 — a different decomposition; at most fifteen lines).**
(1) Greedy steering (round13_q1 Prop CP). Work on a compact Pesin-Egorov block K (round13_q6 Lemma 1.2: uniform local leaves, (L1)/(L2) at rate theta, (L4) by Egorov, non-atomic leaf conditionals). Let Delta(q) = dist(q, W^{cs}_loc(x_0)) measured in a Lyapunov chart in the centre directions E^c_0 (the zero exponents besides X). If from every q in K with small Delta(q) one can find a short su-path inside K (cost <= A Delta^alpha) that reduces Delta by a factor kappa < 1 while avoiding finitely many prescribed points, the concatenation is a summable chain ending in W^{cs}(x_0) with an isolated first vertex; running it twice with disjoint vertex sets gives (SC). Lebesgue density of K on leaves replaces the intermediate-value theorem (Heisenberg model).
(2) Dichotomy via an equivariant "defect cone". For x in K let D(x) be the closed cone in E^c_0(x) of first-order centre defects of small su-quadrilaterals based near x (limits of defect/(product of side lengths)). D is a measurable Dg_1-equivariant family (holonomies commute with the dynamics; zero centre exponents keep the defects from being rescaled exponentially). By ergodicity its linear span has constant dimension k. If span D = E^c_0 and D positively spans, (1) applies. If D = {0} (all first-order defects vanish), the su-holonomies close to first order and, by an invariance-principle argument (zero centre exponents, as in Ledrappier / Avila-Viana: conditional measures along centre plaques are invariant under su-holonomy), quadrilaterals close exactly up to a flow shift on a positive-measure set, giving finite closed chains (two of them at one base point give (SC) through Theorem L / IC2). Intermediate k: quotient by the span and iterate.
(3) What I cannot see: that the defect map has a first-order expansion at all on a fractal block (Pesin leaves are only Hölder in the base point; (SR) of round12_q5), and that the invariance principle applies without a centre foliation. Either a proof of (SC) or a GH-free counterexample (showing the route must use GH in the construction) would be decisive.
