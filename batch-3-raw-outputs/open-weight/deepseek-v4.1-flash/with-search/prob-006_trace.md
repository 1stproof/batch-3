\section{Introduction}

Let $K$ be a knot in $S^3$. A Dehn surgery on $K$ with slope $r \in \mathbb{Q} \cup \{\infty\}$ is the operation of removing a tubular neighborhood of $K$ and gluing it back so that the meridian of the torus is glued to a curve of slope $r$. The resulting 3-manifold is denoted $S^3_r(K)$. A knot $K$ is an \emph{L-space knot} if $S^3_r(K)$ is an L-space for some $r > 0$. L-space knots form an important class in Heegaard Floer homology, and they are known to be fibered and have simple knot Floer homology.

The question we address is: Does $S^3_r(K)$ have at most two prime summands for every L-space knot $K$ and every slope $r$? We answer this question in the affirmative.

Let $K$ be an L-space knot in $S^3$, and let $r$ be any surgery slope. Then $S^3_r(K)$ has at most two prime summands.
\end{theorem}

\end{proof}
