## FINDING 1 (Blocking Side Issue — rigor, does NOT touch the big theorems):
## `lem:allpairs` asserts `Ψ(E_f) = PMCG(A)` via an unstated
## anti-homomorphism extension, and writes `Ψ(x_i)=e` on a plain definition
## whose only proved precursor is `Ψ(x_i^{∘2})=e`

**Where:** solution.tex `lem:allpairs` (lines 606–637), its proof
(~624–637), and the `E_f` definitions at line 455 vs line 607.

**What is wrong — three linked defects.**

(a) *The extension that produces `Ψ(E_f)` is never stated, and it is an
*anti*-homomorphism, not a homomorphism.* `lem:allpairs` defines
`E_f := ⟨x_i, a_j⟩` (a *group*) and claims "`Ψ(E_f) = PMCG(A)`, because
`⟨Ψ(a_0),Ψ(a_2)⟩ = PMCG(A)`". But the composition rule the paper itself
proves (line 466) is the **reversal**
`Ψ(σ_1σ_2) = Ψ(σ_2)Ψ(σ_1)`. `lem:allpairs` nowhere says how Ψ extends to
words; it silently treats "the image of a generated subgroup under Ψ"
as if it were the subgroup generated by the images. For a general
anti-homomorphism this is false. It happens to be *true* here for a
non-obvious reason — on these particular generators, each `Ψ` of a
two-generator word equals the corresponding Ψ-free word because the
two-cycle `a_0 ↔ a_2` (`Ψ(a_0)=u_0`, `Ψ(a_2)=u_2`) agrees with its
reversal and `Ψ(x_i)=e` is reversal-invariant — so the image lands in
`⟨Ψ(a_0),Ψ(a_2)⟩`, which is all of `PMCG(A)`. But *none of this is
written*; the lemma states a set-equality of groups while only proving a
generator-image statement. The reader cannot verify the step without
re-deriving the reversal-coincidence himself. Fix: `lem:allpairs` must
state explicitly that Ψ is the *anti-*homomorphic descended class (citing
the reversal at line 466), and justify that `Ψ(E_f)` — despite the
reversal — equals `⟨Ψ(a_0),Ψ(a_2)⟩` on these generators (e.g. "since
`Ψ(x_i)=e` and the reversal of a word in `a_0,a_2` maps to the reversed
word in `u_0,u_2`, and `⟨u_0,u_2⟩` is closed under reversal, the image
is `⟨u_0,u_2⟩ = PMCG(A)`").

(b) *`Ψ(x_i)=e` is asserted for the plain twist, but only `Ψ(x_i^{∘2})=e`
was proved.* `lem:descend` (line 515, 565) proves the smallest *liftable*
power of `T_{{p_i,v}}` is `m_0=2` and `Ψ(x_i^{∘2})=e`. `lem:allpairs`
(line 610) and `prop:contenttable` (lines 644–647) then write `Ψ(x_i)=e`
*for the unsquared twist*, and even put `x_i ∈ E_f`. But `x_i` itself is
not liftable (the odd power does not lift — `thm:halflift`(ii)
establishes `T_γ=x_2` has no lift); a descended class for a non-liftable
twist is *undefined*. Writing `Ψ(x_i)=e` conflates "the square descends
trivially" with "the twist descends trivially"; the latter is meaningless
as stated. Fix: either define `Ψ` only on the liftable subgroup and write
`Ψ(x_i^{∘2})=e` throughout (and drop `x_i` from the `E_f` generators,
keeping only `x_i^{∘2}`), or explicitly state that `Ψ(x_i)` is defined
*via the lift of its square* with descent degree 2. As written, a
specialist referee flags "`Ψ(x_1)=id` on a twist that does not lift" as an
undefined-quantifier error.

(c) *`\Ef` is used before and inconsistently with its definition.*
`\mathcal E_f` appears at line 429 (`T_γ^{2k+1} ∉ E_f`) and line 449
(`Ψ: E_f → PMCG(A)`) before any definition; line 455 defines
`E_f := ⟨x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3,u_0,u_1,u_2,u_3⟩` (the *non-adjacent* pair
twists `u_i = T_{{p_i,p_3}}`, `u_3 = T_{{p_0,p_2}}`), while line 607
re-defines `E_f := ⟨x_i, a_j⟩` with `a_j = T_{{p_j,p_{j+1}}}` the
*adjacent* cycle-pair twists. These are different generating sets:
`u_1 = T_{{p_1,p_3}}` (used at line 455) appears nowhere else in the
paper and is not among the `a_j`. As subgroups of `PMCG(B)` they differ;
only their images in `PMCG(A)` coincide (by the complementary-pair
identification). A back-reference to `E_f` (e.g. eq:compcrit's
"σ ∈ E_f") is ambiguous about which group is meant. Fix: define `\Ef`
once, early (start of §sec:content), as *the set of mapping classes
liftable along `f`, or the subgroup generated by the liftable pair
twists*, with ONE generator description used consistently; explicitly
reconcile the `u_i`/non-adjacent vs `a_j`/adjacent usage (state that
both generate the same subgroup of `PMCG(A)` under descent, but only if
that is the intended identification).

**Why this is a side issue, not a mainline block:** `Ψ(E_f)=PMCG(A)` is
