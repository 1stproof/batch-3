# Round 0 Contract

## One Mainline Objective

Produce a complete, rigorous, self-contained proof in `solution.tex` of the
statement in `problem.tex`: if $X$ is a globally hypoelliptic smooth vector
field on a compact connected smooth manifold $M$ (every distribution $u$
with $\mathcal L_X u \in C^\infty(M)$ is itself smooth), then the flow $g_t$
generated by $X$ has zero topological entropy.

## Approach (planned proof shape)

1. Reduce to $(6.1)$: under global hypoellipticity (GH) there exists $u \in
   C^\infty$ with $\mathcal L_X u = 1$ (Liouville), citing Frontera da
   Costa--Gramchev (J.~Funct.~Anal. 2001, proof in F.~Treves, "Topological
   Vector Spaces, Distributions and Kernels", Thms 38.2--38.3).
2. $(6.1) \Rightarrow$ all periodic orbits have integer period (integrate
   along orbits: $u \circ g_t - u = t$), and $M$ is a fibre bundle over
   $S^1$ with the fibres of $u$ transverse to $X$, $X$ related to the
   suspension of the return map.
3. Combine with the equivalence of GH with the property that $X$ has no
   periodic orbit and no divergent orbit (F.~Treves, op.~cit., Thm 38.3)
   and the "trichotomy": if (C) holds ($X \neq 0$, no periodic orbit, no
   divergent orbit) then every orbit returns infinitely often to a
   transverse disk around each of its points, hence is wandering
   (contradiction) since $C^\infty$ functions are at most countably many
   while the return intervals are uncountably many; a smooth function
   constant on a connectivity-suitable set of intervals is globally
   constant.
4. Therefore $X = 0$, the flow is the identity for all $t$, and the
   topological entropy of $Z = T^0 M$ is $0$.
