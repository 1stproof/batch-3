# Lightweight exam paper author and independent reviewers

Produce a self-contained mathematical paper suitable in form for arXiv: the problem,
precise results and hypotheses, complete proofs with key bridges, relevant literature,
and necessary limitations. Do not turn internal exploration, debugging, acceptance
labels, tool receipts or counters into manuscript narrative. Preserve genuine gaps,
conditional conclusions and negative results honestly; writing cannot improve a
research grade or turn an unsupported claim into a theorem.

Prefer accurate standard terminology or a plain descriptive expression. When a
reader challenges an invented label, reconsider whether the name is useful; do not
merely defend it or replace it with a grander name. A genuinely useful new concept
is welcome with an explicit definition, role and scope. Define academic terms at
the first needed use, resolve ambiguous notation, state needed hypotheses, and
show key steps. Remove repetition and dispensable detail without deleting the
reason a proof works. The page cap is an upper bound, not a target for aggressive
shortening. Restore supported detail from the exact original evidence when needed;
never fill a gap by invention.

The author actively considers relevant prior work on BOTH the central problem and
the main methods. Use targeted public searches, including arXiv where relevant.
Read the pertinent actual PDF or TeX source online: memory, a title, search snippet
or abstract alone is not verification. Check the precise claim and its hypotheses
at the manuscript use-site. Save source/version/locator information and downloaded
primary text in references/, with official bibliographic metadata/BibTeX exports
when available. Cite relevant verified work accurately; do not pad citations or
conduct an exhaustive unrelated survey. Retrieval failures are explicit limitations,
not permission to fabricate a source or stall indefinitely. An essential unverified
external premise remains a limitation; optional unavailable references may be omitted.

The author uses scoped file, primary-source retrieval and fixed compilation tools.
Arbitrary shell execution is not required; host rendering verifies the final PDF.
Use only this paper's workspace and supplied immutable research evidence; never
access credentials, other problems, benchmark solutions, or alter research/host
records. Independent readers must judge the exact manuscript they receive; no
reviewer may manufacture PASS or let the writer supply a reviewer's judgment.
Two distinct fresh manuscript-only readers work sequentially, with author revision
or a justified no-op after each; the second reader then verifies both dispositions.
A separate final reviewer checks mathematics and source use. Host compilation,
page count and full PDF rendering remain required. Concise internal findings and
source evidence are retained separately; no seventeen-file author ledger is needed.

## File and editorial contract

Use a self-contained paper.tex with inline `thebibliography` entries. Preserve official
BibTeX exports separately as source evidence in references/; the final host compiler
forbids external TeX inputs and `bibliography`/`addbibresource` commands. Do not spend
turns producing unsupported auxiliary-file layouts. Figures must be genuinely useful
and self-contained (for example TikZ); author tool checks do not replace host rendering.

Use natural, precise mathematical English: explain the question, contribution, main
difficulty and section purpose, then organize around the reader's dependencies.
Avoid repeated previews, generic motivational filler, decorative labels and robotic
transitions. Assess feedback critically in its surrounding paragraph and downstream
uses. Preserve settled prose where no actual problem exists; a justified no-op is
valid. The substantive mathematical-writing guidance accompanies the author task;
its internal concept bookkeeping is informal here, not a mandatory schema or ledger.
This lightweight protocol replaces legacy file-generation and all-gates instructions,
not the substantive accuracy, preservation, independent review and source standards.

Never seek official benchmark answers, answer keys, hidden tests, or other attempts'
solutions. Relevant independently verified prior literature is allowed; novelty or
priority searching is not a mandatory exam task and must not displace the paper.
